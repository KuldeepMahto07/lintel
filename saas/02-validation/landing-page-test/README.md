## Page (original copy, Lintel brand)
- **Headline:** Your firm's standards, checked inside the model.
- **Sub:** Lintel reads your Rhino or SketchUp model, flags doors, corridors, stairs and rooms that break your rules, and proposes the fix. Nothing changes until you approve it, and every change is logged.
- **Three proof points:**
  1. Your rules, not a black box. Numbers you set, with the code section you check against.
  2. A diff you approve. See before and after for every edit, then apply or reject.
  3. A record of every decision: who approved what, when and why.
- **CTA:** "Join the pilot". An email plus a "Which tool do you design in?" select (Rhino / SketchUp / Revit / ArchiCAD / other).
- **Demo:** a 20-second GIF from the MVP dashboard (stage 05).

## Test
- Traffic: $300 of LinkedIn ads targeting architects at firms of 1–50 people, plus posts in 3 communities (see stage 10).
- **Measure:** visitor → signup conversion, and the host-tool split (this decides the second host).
- **Pass bar:** ≥ 8% signup on cold traffic, ≥ 150 signups in 4 weeks.

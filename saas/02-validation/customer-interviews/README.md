# Customer interviews

**Status:** 🟡 Script ready; 👤 interviews not run yet

Part of [02 · Validation](../README.md) in the [Lintel SaaS plan](../../README.md).

## Who to talk to (12–15 people)
- 6 project architects or designers at firms of 1–20 people, using Rhino or SketchUp.
- 3 BIM managers or design technologists at firms of 20–100 people (Revit).
- 3 interior designers.
- 1–2 plan reviewers or code consultants, for the reviewer side.

**Where to find them:** LinkedIn (search "project architect" + "Rhino"), the McNeel forum, the SketchUp forum, r/architecture, local AIA chapters, and the owner's own network.

## Script (30 min, don't pitch until the end)
1. Walk me through the last time you checked a design against code or your office standards. What did you open? How long did it take?
2. What did you miss last time, and when was it caught (internal review, plan check, site)? What did it cost?
3. Where do your office standards live? Who keeps them up to date?
4. Have you used scripts, plugins or ChatGPT for this? What stopped you from using them more?
5. If a tool proposed a change in your model, what would it need to show you before you'd click "apply"?
6. Who decides on software purchases at your firm? What do you pay for add-ons today?
7. *(Show the demo GIF: flag → diff → approve.)* What would make this a must-have? What's missing?
8. Would you join a paid pilot at $X/month? (Test $29, $49 and $79 across interviews.)

## Record per interview
Role, firm size, host app, hours per week on checks, top 3 rules they care about, trust requirements, willingness to pay, and quotes.

**Success bar:** at least 8 of 12 say checks are ≥ 2 h/week, and at least 5 agree to a pilot.

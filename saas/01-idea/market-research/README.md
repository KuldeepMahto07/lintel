# Market research

**Status:** 🟡 Partial: desk sizing done, numbers to verify

Part of [01 · Idea](../README.md) in the [Lintel SaaS plan](../../README.md).

## Market size (desk estimates; verify before investor use)
- **Architecture firms:** about 100k in the US (AIA firm survey, order of magnitude), with hundreds of thousands of licensed architects worldwide. Most firms are under 10 people.
- **Host install base** (vendor figures, rounded):
  - Revit dominates large and mid firms.
  - SketchUp has a very large base in small firms, interiors and residential.
  - Rhino is strong in design-led practices and schools.
  - ArchiCAD is strong in Europe and residential.
- **Spend:** BIM seats already cost $2–3k/yr (Revit) or $350–750/yr (SketchUp Pro/Studio, Rhino perpetual about $1k). A $30–100/seat/month add-on is within normal tooling budgets if it saves a few hours a month.

## Signals
- Plug-in marketplaces (Autodesk App Store, Food4Rhino, SketchUp Extension Warehouse) carry hundreds of QA and productivity add-ons. Firms install add-ons.
- AI-for-AEC funding rounds in 2024–26 (UpCodes, Snaptrude, Augmenta and others) show investor appetite.
- Heron itself is an agency concept: there's demand for the story, and no shipped product yet.

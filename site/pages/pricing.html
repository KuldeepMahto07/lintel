Questions

About plans and billing.

Is there a trial of the paid plans?

Yes, 14 days of Pro with no card. When it ends you drop to Free and keep your rules and history.

What counts as a seat?

A person who approves or applies changes. Viewing the dashboard doesn't need a paid seat.

Can I change plans or cancel?

Any time. Upgrades are prorated, and cancelling keeps you on Free with your data intact.

Is there a discount for the pilot?

Founding firms in the pilot get Pro for up to 3 seats at $390 a year, locked for as long as they stay.

Do you offer education pricing?

Yes: free seats for students and teaching staff at accredited architecture schools.

Questions

About the dashboard.

Can the whole team use the same dashboard?

Yes, on the Firm plan. Everyone sees the same projects, approval queue and history, and each approval is recorded under the person who made it.

Does it keep a record of past changes?

Every proposal, approval, rejection, applied edit, revert and rule change is logged with a time, a person and a reason.

Can I bring in our office standards?

Yes. Paste or upload a CSV or JSON file of rules: a name, the check, the value and the reference. Edit them any time.

Where is my model data kept?

During the pilot Lintel can run entirely on your own computer. The hosted version stores model snapshots per firm and sends only what a request needs to the AI provider.
